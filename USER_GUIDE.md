## 1. Getting Started

When you open JournalInsight for the first time, you'll be asked for your name. This is used to personalize the greeting on the main screen. You can change your name at any time in **Settings**.

The app starts with two sample entries so the dashboard isn't empty on first launch. You can delete these at any time.

---

## 2. Dashboard

The dashboard is your home screen. It shows:

- A personalized greeting ("Welcome Back, [Name]!")
- A **widget grid** with quick-access tiles for each section
- A **recent entries list** below the widgets

### Widget Grid

Five widgets are displayed by default:

| Widget | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Streak Info** | Your current consecutive journaling streak |
| **Session KPIs** | Total number of journal entries |
| **Questions Overview** | Quick access to journaling prompts |
| **Calendar Overview** | Entries written this week |
| **Goals Gantt Chart** | Quick access to your goals |

Tap any widget to open that section in full.

### Customizing Widgets

**Resize a widget:** Long-press (or right-click) a widget card and choose **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** from the context menu. Small widgets pair side-by-side; Medium and Large take the full width.

**Reorder widgets:** Drag a widget card and drop it onto another to swap their positions. The new layout is saved automatically.

## 3. Writing Journal Entries

Tap the **+** button (top-right of the dashboard) to open the New Entry sheet.

### Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Date** | Defaults to today. Tap to change to a past or future date. |
| **Mood** | Choose one of five moods: 😄 Great, 🙂 Good, 😐 Okay, 😞 Bad, 😢 Terrible. Tap again to deselect. |
| **Journal Text** | The body of your entry. Required to save. |
| **Duration** | How long you journaled. Use the slider (1–120 min) or enable the live timer. |
| **Tags** | Categorize your entry. Tap existing tags to toggle them, or type a new tag name and tap **Add**. |

### Using the Timer

Toggle **Use Timer** to start a built-in stopwatch:

1. Tap **Start** to begin counting.
2. Tap **Pause** to pause.
3. Tap **Reset** to zero out the time.
4. When you tap **Save**, the elapsed time is recorded as the entry duration.

If you cancel the sheet while the timer is running, it stops automatically.

### Saving

Tap **Save** in the top-right corner. The entry is saved immediately and appears in your recent entries list.

## 4. Searching Entries

Tap the **search bar** at the top of the dashboard (or pull down on the entry list) to search your entries.

The search matches against:
- Entry **text** (full-text, case-insensitive)
- **Mood** label (e.g. "great", "okay")
- **Tag** names

Results update in real time as you type. Clear the search bar to return to the recent entries view.

---

## 5. Editing and Deleting Entries

Long-press (or right-click) any entry row to reveal the context menu:

- **Edit** — opens the Edit Entry sheet with all fields pre-populated. Adjust any field and tap **Save**.
- **Delete** — shows a confirmation dialog before permanently removing the entry.

Swipe-to-delete is not available; use the context menu.

## 6. Calendar View

Access from the **Calendar Overview** widget on the dashboard.

### Navigation

- Tap **‹** or **›** to move backward or forward in time.
- Tap **Today** to jump back to the current date.

### View Scopes

Use the segmented picker to switch between:

| Scope | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| Month | Full calendar month grid |
| Work Week | Monday–Friday of the current week |
| Full Week | All 7 days of the current week |
| 2 Weeks | Two weeks centered on the current week |
| 4 Weeks | Four weeks centered on the current week |

### Entry Indicators

Days with entries show a small indicator below the date number:
- **Mood emoji** (e.g. 😄) if the entry has a mood recorded
- **Teal dot** if the entry has no mood

### Viewing a Day's Entries

Tap any date to select it. Entries written on that day appear in a scrollable list below the grid, showing the mood, text preview, duration, and tags.

## 7. Streak Tracking

Access from the **Streak Info** widget.

A streak counts how many consecutive days you've written at least one entry. The streak breaks if you miss a day.

### What You'll See

| Stat | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Current Streak** | Days in your active streak (shown large, with a flame icon) |
| **Best Streak** | Longest streak you've ever achieved |
| **Most Active** | The time of day you most commonly journal (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night) |
| **Total Entries** | All-time entry count |

### Milestones

JournalInsight tracks these milestones: **7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365 days**.

- Completed milestones show a green checkmark.
- A progress bar shows how far you are toward the next milestone.
- When you open the streak view on a milestone day, a **celebration banner** animates in at the top.

## 8. Session KPIs

Access from the **Session KPIs** widget.

This screen shows statistics and charts for your journaling habits.

### Overview Stats

| Stat | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Entries | All-time entry count |
| Total Time | Sum of all entry durations |
| Average Duration | Mean entry duration |
| Longest Session | Longest single entry duration |
| This Week | Entries written in the current calendar week |

### Charts

Three charts visualize your recent activity:

1. **Entries This Week** — bar chart of daily entry counts for the past 7 days.
2. **Duration Trend** — line chart of total journaling time per day for the past 7 days.
3. **Mood Distribution** — bar chart showing how often each mood was recorded across all entries.

Below the charts, the 5 most recent entries are listed with mood, date, and text preview.

## 9. Journaling Prompts

Access from the **Questions Overview** widget.

The prompts screen offers guided questions to spark your writing when you're not sure what to write about.

### Today's Prompts

Three prompts are highlighted at the top each day. These rotate daily so you see fresh prompts each time you open the app.

### All Prompts

All 10 prompts are listed below for on-demand access.

### Responding to a Prompt

Tap any prompt to open a response sheet:

1. The selected prompt appears at the top for reference.
2. Select your current **mood** (optional).
3. Write your response in the **Your Response** text area.
4. Tap **Save** to create a journal entry. The prompt text is prepended to your response automatically.

## 10. Goals

Access from the **Goals Gantt Chart** widget.

Goals let you track personal objectives with a target date and progress indicator.

### Adding a Goal

Tap **+** in the toolbar, enter a title and target date, then tap **Save**.

### Tracking Progress

Each goal row shows:
- Goal title
- A color-coded progress bar
- Target date
- A stepper to adjust progress in 10% increments

Progress bar colors:
- **Green** — goal is complete (100%)
- **Red** — goal is overdue (target date has passed and not yet complete)
- **Teal** — goal is in progress

### Deleting a Goal

Swipe left on a goal row and tap **Delete**.

## 11. Settings & Personalization

Access via the **gear icon** (⚙) in the dashboard toolbar.

### Name

Change your display name. Tap **Save** to apply. A "Saved!" confirmation appears briefly.

### Wallpaper

Set a photo as your personal wallpaper:
1. Tap **Select New Wallpaper** to open your photo library.
2. Choose a photo.
3. Tap the thumbnail to preview it full-screen.
4. Tap **Remove Wallpaper** (red) to revert to the default background.

### Accent Color

Choose from 6 accent colors: Teal, Blue, Purple, Orange, Pink, Green. The selected color is applied to buttons, progress bars, and interactive elements throughout the app.

### Text Size

Choose from 4 text sizes: Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large. This scales all text in the app.

### Appearance

Choose between **System** (follows your device's Dark/Light Mode setting), **Light**, or **Dark**.

### Daily Reminder

Enable a daily push notification to remind you to journal:

1. Toggle **Enable Reminder** to on. You'll be prompted to allow notifications if you haven't already.
2. If allowed, set your preferred **Reminder Time** using the hour and minute pickers.

> **Note:** If you previously denied notification permission, the toggle will silently revert. To fix this, go to **Settings.app > Notifications > JournalInsight** and enable notifications there.

### iCloud Sync

JournalInsight is designed to sync data automatically via iCloud when a CloudKit container is configured in the app. No additional setup is required on your part once the app is fully configured for your account.

## 12. Exporting Your Data

In **Settings**, under **Export Data**:

1. Choose a format: **CSV** (opens in spreadsheet apps) or **JSON** (for developers/backup tools).
2. Tap **Export [N] Entries**.
3. A share sheet appears — save to Files, email it, or share via any app.

### What's Included

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `date` | ISO 8601 timestamp of the entry |
| `text` | Full entry text |
| `duration_seconds` | Duration in seconds |
| `mood` | Mood label (e.g. "great", "okay"), if set |
| `tags` | Semicolon-separated tag names (CSV) / array (JSON) |

> **Note:** Goals are not included in the export. Tags and their relationships are included inline with each entry.

## 13. Tips & Tricks

- **Quick mood search** — type "great" or "terrible" in the search bar to filter entries by mood.
- **Backfill old entries** — use the date picker in the New Entry sheet to add entries for past days.
- **Timer for timed journaling** — use the built-in timer to practice timed free-writing sessions.
- **Tag consistently** — using consistent tag names (e.g. "work", "gratitude") makes search much more powerful over time.
- **Check your streak daily** — the Streak widget on the dashboard shows your current streak at a glance so you never accidentally break a streak.
- **Milestone days** — open the Streak screen on a milestone day (7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365) to see the celebration animation.

---

*JournalInsight stores all data locally on your device. Your journal entries, moods, tags, and goals are private and never transmitted to any server.*
